Smoked Salmon Potato Chowder
2 or 3 servings
2 tablespoons butter Melt in a soup pot over medium-low heat.
6 oz onion, diced
3-4 oz celery, diced Add and cook 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until
softened but not browned.
15 oz russet potato, diced into 1/2-inch pieces
2 cups water
3/4 teaspoon Better Than Bouillon vegetable base Add, stir well, bring to a
gentle simmer, and cook 15-20 minutes, until the potato is soft.
  Mash about one-third to one-half of the potato in the pot, leaving the rest
chunky.
1 cup half-and-half
1/2 cup milk
1/4 teaspoon black pepper
1/4 teaspoon dried thyme or marjoram Lower the heat, add, and warm gently
3-5 minutes. Do not boil hard after adding dairy.
8 oz fully cooked smoked salmon, broken into bite-size pieces Add at the
end and warm gently 2-3 minutes, just until heated through.
Salt Taste first, then add only if needed. If the soup is too thick, add a splash
of milk or half-and-half.
