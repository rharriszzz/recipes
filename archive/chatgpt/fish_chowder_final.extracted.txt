Fish Chowder
Ingredients (2–3 servings)

2–3 strips bacon, chopped

1 tbsp butter

1 cup onion, finely chopped

½ cup celery, finely chopped

1 tbsp flour

1¼ cups water

¾ tsp Better Than Bouillon vegetable base

1 bay leaf

¼–½ tsp dried thyme

1½ cups potatoes, peeled and diced small

0.6 lb haddock, cut in bite-size pieces

¾ cup half-and-half

Black pepper, to taste
Day 1 – Build the Base

Cook bacon until crisp; remove pieces and leave the fat in the pot.

Add butter, then onion and celery; cook 10–12 min until lightly browned.

Sprinkle in flour; stir and cook 1 minute.

Add water, bouillon, bay leaf, and thyme.

Add potatoes; simmer 12–15 min until just tender.

Simmer uncovered 3–5 min to concentrate the base.

Cool and refrigerate overnight.
Day 2 – Finish

Reheat gently, 5–10 min.

Add haddock; simmer 3–4 min.

Add half-and-half; heat gently. Do not boil.

Return bacon; remove bay leaf.

Taste; add pepper and salt only if needed.
Notes

Bouillon plus bacon may provide enough salt already.

Mash a few potatoes into the broth if you want it thicker.

Optional at the end: a tiny splash of lemon juice or a pinch of paprika.
