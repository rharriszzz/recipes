You already have an excellent base for a cod, potato, leek, and celery chowder. I’d keep the seasoning fairly restrained so the cod stays prominent. Thyme and a little smoked paprika will work especially well.

Cod and Potato Chowder
About 4 generous servings
You already have:
6 stalks celery + 1 large Vidalia onion + 1 leek, chopped and softened in ½ stick (4 Tbsp) butter
Keep this in the pot over medium-low heat.
2 Tbsp flour
Sprinkle over the softened vegetables. Stir and cook for about 2 minutes. You aren't really browning or “toasting” it—you are cooking the flour in the butter and vegetables to make a light roux.
½ tsp dried thyme
¼ tsp smoked paprika
¼ tsp black pepper
1 bay leaf, if you have one
Stir into the vegetables for about 30 seconds. I would not use much more smoked paprika; cod is delicate.
1½ lb potatoes, peeled or unpeeled, cut into roughly ½-inch cubes
Stir them into the vegetables.
2 cups water or chicken broth
½ tsp Better Than Bouillon, optional if using water
Add enough liquid to nearly cover the potatoes. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover partially, and cook about 12–15 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
At this point, taste the broth. If it seems thin, crush several potato pieces against the side of the pot and stir them back in. That gives you a more chowder-like texture without adding more flour.
1½ cups whole milk
1 cup half-and-half
Stir them in and heat gently until the chowder is steaming. Do not boil it vigorously once the dairy is in.
1 lb cod, cut into large 1- to 1½-inch pieces
Lay the cod pieces into the hot chowder. Stir very gently. Keep at a bare simmer for about 5–7 minutes, until the cod has turned opaque and flakes easily.
Salt, to taste
Another ¼ tsp thyme, if desired
Black pepper, to taste
Remove the bay leaf and taste before adding much salt, particularly if you used broth or Better Than Bouillon.

I would not add oregano, rosemary, or a lot of mixed seasoning to this one. Your leek/onion/celery mixture is already quite flavorful. Thyme + black pepper + just a background note of smoked paprika should give you a particularly good cod chowder. One change I might make depending on how much vegetable mixture you actually have: six celery stalks plus a large onion and leek is a substantial amount. If the pot looks very vegetable-heavy after adding 1½ lb potatoes, increase the liquid to about 2½ cups broth/water + 2 cups milk + 1 cup half-and-half. The recipe is forgiving.
