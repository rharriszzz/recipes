Dolsot Bibimbap

Adapted from Seonkyoung Longest's Dolsot Bibimbap and My Korean Kitchen's Dolsot Bibimbap and Daikon Radish Salad.

Bibimbap Sauce

1 Tbsp gochujang

1 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil

1 1/2 tsp sugar

1 1/2 tsp water

1 1/2 tsp toasted sesame seeds

1/2 tsp rice vinegar or apple-cider vinegar

1/2 tsp minced garlic

Mix until smooth. Cover and refrigerate. This can be made several days ahead.

Daikon Radish Salad (Musaengchae)

8 oz daikon radish, peeled and cut into thin matchsticks

Put the radish in a mixing bowl.

1 tsp gochugaru

1 tsp Korean fish sauce

1 tsp sugar

1/2 tsp minced garlic

1/4 tsp fine salt

Add to the radish and mix well.

2 tsp finely chopped green onion (optional)

Mix in. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes. This makes more than you need for two bibimbap bowls; keep the extra as banchan.

Spinach

5 oz fresh spinach

Blanch in boiling water for about 30 seconds. Drain, cool under cold water, squeeze gently, and cut into manageable lengths.

1 tsp toasted sesame oil

1 tsp finely chopped green onion

1/2 tsp minced garlic

1/2 tsp toasted sesame seeds

small pinch salt

Mix with the spinach. Cover and refrigerate.

Bean Sprouts

4 oz soybean sprouts or mung-bean sprouts

Cook until just tender, then drain and cool. Soybean sprouts take longer than mung-bean sprouts.

1 tsp toasted sesame oil

1 tsp finely chopped green onion

1/2 tsp minced garlic

1/2 tsp toasted sesame seeds

small pinch salt

Mix with the sprouts. Cover and refrigerate.

Sauteed Vegetables

1 tsp neutral cooking oil

2 oz carrot, julienned

small pinch salt

Saute over medium-high heat for 2 to 3 minutes. Remove to a container.

1 tsp neutral cooking oil

2 oz zucchini, julienned

small pinch salt

In the same skillet, saute for 2 to 3 minutes. Remove to a container.

1 tsp neutral cooking oil

2 oz fresh shiitake mushrooms, thinly sliced

small pinch salt

In the same skillet, saute for 4 to 5 minutes. Remove to a container. Refrigerate all three vegetables.

Beef

6 oz ribeye, sirloin, or New York strip, thinly sliced

1 Tbsp soy sauce

1 1/2 tsp mirin

1 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil

1 Tbsp sugar

black pepper

Mix well. Cover and refrigerate for at least 20 minutes; it may be prepared the day before.

Kimchi

2 oz prepared napa-cabbage kimchi

Cut into bite-size pieces if necessary. Refrigerate.

Rice and Stone Pots

1 cup uncooked Korean or Japanese short-grain white rice

water for cooking

Cook the rice. You need about 2 1/2 cups cooked rice for the two bowls.

2 stone dolsot bowls

Put the empty dolsots in a cold oven. Set the oven to 425 F. When the oven reaches temperature, heat the bowls for another 10 to 15 minutes.

Beef and Eggs

marinated beef

Cook in a skillet over medium-high to high heat for about 4 to 6 minutes, until cooked through and lightly browned. Set aside.

1 tsp neutral cooking oil

2 large eggs

Fry the eggs sunny-side-up, or to your preference. Set aside briefly.

ASSEMBLE

about 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil, divided

Carefully remove the hot dolsots from the oven. Brush the bottom and lower sides of each bowl with sesame oil.

about 1 1/4 cups hot cooked rice per bowl

Add the rice and press it gently against the bottom and lower sides.

sauteed carrot

sauteed zucchini

sauteed shiitake mushrooms

spinach

bean sprouts

daikon radish salad

kimchi

cooked beef

Arrange small portions of the toppings over the rice. You do not have to use all of every topping.

2 fried eggs

Put one egg on each bowl.

the two assembled dolsots

Return to the 425 F oven for about 8 to 12 minutes, until very hot and the rice is beginning to crisp. If you want a darker crust next time, increase the final oven time a little.

prepared bibimbap sauce

toasted sesame seeds, if desired

Set each dolsot on a substantial trivet. Add sauce to taste, sprinkle with sesame seeds if desired, mix thoroughly, and serve.
