Enchiladas and Posole Soup

2 large slices of a sweet onion
1 clove of garlic, peeled
15 whole black peppercorns
two teaspoons salt
2 chicken breasts including the skin and bone, about 3 pounds, rinsed
  add these ingredients to a large pot, then add water to cover
  bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, and cover
  simmer for 90 minutes, then turn off the heat, and let it cool
  remove the chicken from the broth
  strain the broth, discard the solids, and reserve the broth for the soup
(you can refrigerate or freeze the chicken and the broth at this point)

Prepare the roasted peppers
  preheat the oven to 400 or 425
a large red bell pepper and a large green bell pepper and/or 6 poblano peppers, rinsed
  put the peppers on a tray and roast for 10 minutes
  rotate the peppers and roast for 5 more minutes
  and repeat until the peppers are getting dark and the skin is peeling
  then remove the seeds, peel off the skin, and dice.
Instead of roasting the peppers yourself, you can use
one 16 oz jar of roasted, peeled peppers, chopped

one large sweet onion, peeled and chopped
  sautee the onion in
one tablespoon of oil until it is very soft, but not yet brown
  add the onions to the peppers
(you can refrigerate the peppers and onions at this point)

one or two cups of coarsely grated monterey jack cheese

remove the skin and bone from the chicken, and tear it into bite sized shreads
reserve some of the chicken (between 1/2 and 1/3 of the total) for the soup.
reserve some of the peppers and onions for the soup.
mix everything else together with the cheese.

one package white corn tortillas
  get a small pan, wide enough for one tortilla, but not much wider. Add
1/8 inch oil, heat very hot.
  fry each tortilla for 5 seconds on one side, then 3 seconds on the other.
  we will use about 12 tortillas for the enchiladas,
  if you make more, the rest will be chopped and will go in the soup.

one 28 oz can of red enchilada sauce
  take a small part of the sauce,
  and put it in the bottom of two glass baking pans,
  and spread it around.

Take a tortilla, fill it with the chicken, peppers, and cheese,
and roll it up and put it in the pan. Repeat until the pans are full.
pour the rest of the enchilada sauce over top of the enchiladas.
any of the chicken mixture that is left over will go in the soup.

If you refrigerated the broth, you can (try to) remove the fat on top.
  add the remaining chicken, onions, peppers, then take
One can posole, pour the contents into a bowl, and stir it to separate, and add it to the soup
take any fried tortillas that were not used, and chop them into shreds, and add them to the soup
Add 2 to 4 tablespoons of
Chile Pasilla de Oaxaca Salsa, sold by "Rosa Mexicano kitchen"
  or add a little chipotle powder and a little of some other salsa.
Bring the soup to a boil, and reduce to a simmer.
Taste the soup, and add a little salt, if it needs it.

Preheat the oven to 350, then bake the enchiladas for 15 to 20 minutes.
remove the enchiladas from the oven, and cover the pan with foil

serve the soup, along with
finely shredded cabbage for garnish
