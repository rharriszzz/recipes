Waffles

I started with a recipe from The Fannie Farmer Cookbook, and added some sugar and spice.

4 eggs
2 cups milk
6 tablespoons oil
1 teaspoon vanilla
  beat together

  use another bowl for the dry ingredients
2 tablespoons baking powder
4 tablespoons sugar
1 teaspoon salt
1 teaspoon cinnamon
  mix together
3 cups whole wheat flour
  mix in 1/2 cup of the flour, then mix in the of the flour

  then mix everything together

  heat the waffle iron. if it isn't teflon, brush it with oil or shortening.
  pour in some of the batter, and bake.
